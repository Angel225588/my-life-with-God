# The Scoreboard

Most people track everything and change nothing. I'm tracking **one number**, and
three supporting ones.

---

## THE NUMBER

> ## Real conversations with a decision-maker, per week: **10**

A real conversation = a human who can say yes, who heard the offer, and who
responded. Not an email sent. Not a view. Not a like.

I control this number completely. It doesn't depend on luck, algorithms, or
anyone's mood. If I hit 10 a week for a year, the money is a mathematical
consequence.

**If I only ever look at one line in this repo, it's that one.**

---

## The three supporting numbers

| | Target | Why |

worked, new repos. All of them can go up while the business goes nowhere.

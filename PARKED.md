# Parked — Not Dead

These are good ideas. That's exactly why they're parked: they deserve a version
of me that isn't split six ways.

Each one has a **written wake-up condition**. When the condition is met, it wakes
up. Until then, it doesn't get a single hour — and I don't get to feel guilty
about it either.

---

## Raizane — one system for entrepreneurs (finance, legal, tax)

**Status:** `raizane` + `raizane-proto` on Vercel, June 2026. Paused.

**Why it's parked:** It is the largest idea I have and the furthest from a paying
customer. It touches finance, legal, and tax — three regulated, trust-heavy,
liability-heavy domains. Built solo, without revenue, it is a two-year project
that would eat every other thing I own.

**The reframe that makes this bearable:**
Raizane is not a different idea from iMarketing. It is iMarketing widened.
*"Get businesses out of their paperwork"* — I'm just starting with one vertical
(hospitality) and one document (the breakfast list) instead of all businesses and
all documents at once.

**Every hotel I serve this year is Raizane research.** So I'm not delaying it —
I'm doing the only version of it that can actually work. Keep a running note of
every non-breakfast admin pain a customer mentions. That list *is* the Raizane
roadmap, and it will be written by real customers instead of by me guessing.

**Wake-up condition:** iMarketing at €5,000 MRR sustained for 3 months,
**and** at least 10 customers have asked for something outside hospitality ops.

## Vox — practice speaking without judgment

**Status:** `landing-page-voxlanguage` (May 2025), `v0-language-waitlist-page`
(Oct 2025). Paused.

**Why it's parked:** It's a community/marketplace product. Those need liquidity —
enough people on both sides at the same time — and liquidity needs either
sustained attention or money. I have neither right now.

**Why it will be good:** the pivot is right. It stopped being a language app and
became a *no-judgment room where mistakes are the point*. That's a real need, and
it's closer to my faith than to my business — it's hospitality for people who are
afraid to speak.

**When it wakes, the first move is NOT code.** It's one free 60-minute practice
call, on Zoom or Discord, with 8 people from my audience. Zero build. If 8 people
show up twice, it's real. If they don't, I saved a year.

**Wake-up condition:** iMarketing at €5,000 MRR **and** I have an audience of
1,000+ who'd actually show up to a call. Both, not either.

## POD — print on demand + the design generator

**Status:** `v0-node-canvas-with-api` (Oct 2025) — the design generation system,
unfinished.

**Why it's parked:** Low margin, high volume, and completely at the mercy of
platform algorithms. It needs constant new designs and constant ad spend to reach
5K. It doesn't compound, and it shares nothing with the customers, skills, or
relationships iMarketing is building.

**Why it isn't dead:** the design engine is genuinely valuable, and it's the one
thing here that could become real passive income. It's also the best candidate to
hand to someone else — it's a system, not a relationship.

**Wake-up condition:** iMarketing at €3,000 MRR **and** I have 4 free hours a
week **and** I can pay someone else to run it. Not before. This is a delegation
project, not a founder project.

## The test for any new idea

Before anything moves out of [`IDEAS.md`](IDEAS.md), it has to beat this:

1. Can I sell it to a customer I **already have**?
2. Does it get me to 5K **faster** than putting the same hours into iMarketing?
3. Am I drawn to it because it's better — or because it's *new*, and new feels
   like relief when selling is hard?

Question 3 is the honest one. It's usually the answer.

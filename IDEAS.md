# Idea Inbox

Ideas are not the problem. Ideas arriving **during working hours and winning** is
the problem.

So: when one hits, it comes here. Two minutes, maximum. Then back to work.

**Nothing leaves this file except on a Friday**, and only if it beats the three
questions at the bottom of [`PARKED.md`](PARKED.md).

---

## Format

```
### [date] Short name
What it is, in one line.
Who pays for it:
Why now (or why not):
```

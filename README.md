# My Life With God

> "Commit to the LORD whatever you do, and he will establish your plans." — Proverbs 16:3

This repo is where I keep my direction, so my days have somewhere to point.

## The North Star

**Life goal:** love God, share the good news, and spend my working hours helping
people get free of the friction that steals their time — so they can do what
they were actually made to do.

**Money goal:** 5K/month, active or passive, so that money stops being the thing
that decides my calendar.

## The One Sentence

> I help hotels and restaurants get out of the paperwork — and I tell the truth
> about God while I do it.

That is the whole business right now. Everything else is parked, not dead.

## How to use this repo

| File | What it's for | When I open it |
|---|---|---|
| [`LIFE-PLAN.md`](LIFE-PLAN.md) | The strategy, the money math, the 90-day arc | Once a month |
| [`DAILY.md`](DAILY.md) | The actual daily and weekly rhythm | **Every morning** |
| [`SCOREBOARD.md`](SCOREBOARD.md) | The numbers I'm allowed to care about | Every Friday |
| [`PARKED.md`](PARKED.md) | Raizane, Vox, POD — and exactly when each wakes up | When I'm tempted |
| [`IDEAS.md`](IDEAS.md) | Where new ideas go so they don't hijack today | Whenever one hits |

## The rule this whole repo exists to enforce

**One thing at a time, finished, before the next thing starts.**

Not because the other ideas are bad. Because they're good, and good ideas
deserve a version of me that isn't split six ways.

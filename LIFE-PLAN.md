# The Life Plan

Written 14 Aug 2026. Reviewed the first Friday of every month.

---

## 0. First things first

The business is not the point. The business is what pays for and creates the
platform for the point.

So these come before revenue, and they don't move when the week gets hard:

- **20 minutes in the Word before I open a laptop.** Not for content. For me.
- **Sunday is off.** Actual rest. Church, family, no building, no selling.
  A man who can't stop for one day is being run by something that isn't God.
- **Give from the first money, not the leftover money.** Decide the percentage
  once, then stop deciding it.
- **The way I sell is the witness.** No pressure, no exaggeration, no chasing a
  hotel that clearly doesn't need it. Honest work in front of people who are
  watching is preaching that doesn't need a microphone.

If the money comes and these are gone, I lost.

## 1. The honest diagnosis

15 months. 16 deployed projects. Zero paying customers.

That's not laziness — most people never ship once. It's that **building is the
part I'm good at, so it's the part I run to when selling feels uncomfortable.**
Every new project is a way to feel productive without risking a "no."

The most recent evidence: in June 2026 I started `raizane` — the biggest, most
complex, furthest-from-revenue idea I have — while `check-in`, the finished
sellable one, sat untouched since March.

**The next 90 days contain no new products.** None. The skill I'm building is
not a technical one.

## 2. The thesis that connects everything

I keep starting things that look unrelated. They aren't:

| Project | Who it frees | From what |
|---|---|---|
| check-in / iMarketing | Hotels & restaurants | Paperwork and clipboards |
| Raizane | Entrepreneurs | Finance, legal, tax admin |
| Vox | People learning to speak | Fear of being judged |
| POD | Me | Trading hours for money |

**One mission: remove the friction that stops people from doing what they're for.**

Three expressions of it. They are not competitors — they are a *sequence*.
Raizane is not a different idea from iMarketing; it is iMarketing widened after
it wins in one vertical. Every hotel I serve this year is research for Raizane.

Narrow → win → widen. In that order, or none of them happen.

## 3. The pick: iMarketing / check-in

**Why this one and not the others:**

- It is the only thing already built and demoable **today**.
- Hotels have money, a budget line for operations, and a pain they already know
  they have. I don't have to educate the market.
- It's B2B recurring revenue — it compounds. POD doesn't. Content doesn't.
- I can walk into a hotel lobby with a tablet and show it in 4 minutes. Nothing
  else I own can be sold that way.
- It is the on-ramp to Raizane, which is the thing I actually dream about.

**Everything else is in [`PARKED.md`](PARKED.md) with a written wake-up condition.**

## 4. The offer (stop tweaking the product, sharpen this)

> **"Breakfast lists without the clipboard."**
> Your staff stop chasing paper. You see who ate, when, and how many — live, on
> any screen. We set it up, we train your team, you never think about it again.

**Pricing:**

| | Setup (one time) | Monthly |
|---|---|---|
| Founding 5 (locked forever) | €300 | €49 |
| Standard, from #6 | €600 | €89 |
| Group (3+ properties) | €1,200 | €69 / property |

The setup fee is not greed — it's survival. It pays me *this month* while the
subscriptions compound. A solo founder with only MRR starves for a year.

**Founding customers pay.** Discounted, but paid. Free pilots produce polite
lies; a €300 invoice produces the truth.

## 5. The money math

Target: **5,000/month.** Adjust currency to my market.

At standard price, ARPU ≈ €89 → **56 properties.** That's a lot.
So two levers make it reachable:

1. **Setup fees carry the near term.** 5 setups/month at €600 = €3,000/month in
   cash while MRR is still small.
2. **Raise ARPU with a second module** once 15+ properties are live — whatever
   the customers themselves ask for most (housekeeping, incidents, stock,
   shift notes). At €149 ARPU, 5K needs **34 properties**, not 56.
3. **Groups are worth 5 singles for the same effort.** One 6-property chain is
   3.5% of the goal from a single relationship.

**The realistic path:**

| Milestone | By | Live properties | MRR | + setup cash |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| First paid customer | 27 Aug 2026 | 1 | €49 | €300 |
| Founding 5 done | 27 Sep 2026 | 5 | €245 | €1,500 |
| Proof it's a business | 11 Nov 2026 | 12 | €800 | ~€4,000 total |
| Half way | Feb 2027 | 25 | €2,000 | ongoing |
| **Goal** | Jul–Sep 2027 | 34–50 | **€5,000** | — |

Roughly a year. That's not slow — that's what real is. Sixteen projects in
fifteen months produced €0, so a year to 5K is infinitely faster than what I've
been doing.

> Note: 5K gross is not 5K in my pocket. Taxes, tools, and Vercel come out
> first. Write down the number I actually need to live, and target *that*.

## 6. The 90-day arc

### Days 1–14 — Sharpen and open the door (14–27 Aug)

- Rewrite the check-in landing page to say the offer above. One page. One hour.
  No redesign.
- Make a list of **60 hotels and restaurants** with breakfast service within
  reach of me. Name, address, who's in charge.
- Build a 4-minute demo I can do from a phone or tablet, half asleep.
- **Walk into 20 of them.** Not email. Walk in, between 10am and noon when
  breakfast service is over and the manager can breathe.
- Goal: **1 paying founding customer.**

### Days 15–45 — Founding five (28 Aug – 27 Sep)

- 10 real conversations per week, every week, no exceptions.
- Onboard each customer in person. Watch them use it. Write down every complaint.
- Build **only** what a paying customer asked for out loud.
- Ask each one: *"Who else do you know that has this same problem?"* Referrals in
  hospitality are the whole game.
- Goal: **5 paying properties + 2 written testimonials.**

### Days 46–90 — Prove it repeats (28 Sep – 11 Nov)

- Raise to standard price for new customers. The founding 5 keep theirs.
- Approach one hotel **group** and one hospitality association.
- Ship the second module — whichever one the first five asked for most.
- Goal: **12 paying properties, ~€800 MRR, one group in conversation.**

## 7. The content engine

Content is not a separate project. It's the same project with a camera on.

**One account. One voice. Whole person.** I am not going to run a business
account and a faith account — that's two engines and I've already proven I can't
feed two of anything. People buy from people, and the man who talks about God is
the same man who fixes your breakfast list.

**Cadence: 5 videos a week.** Filmed in one batch on Monday if that's easier.

- **3 about the work** — a hotel problem, a thing I saw, a 30-second demo, a
  customer story. Never "buy my app." Always "here's a problem you have."
- **2 about the life** — what I read that morning, what God is teaching me, what
  I got wrong last week. Honest, short, not preachy.

**Rules so this doesn't eat the business:**
- 45 minutes a day, maximum. It is the third block, never the first.
- Never edit for more than 10 minutes. Bad lighting that ships beats a perfect
  video that doesn't.
- Views are not the scoreboard. **Conversations started** is the scoreboard.

The first 60 videos are tuition. Pay it and stop checking the numbers.

## 8. What "done" looks like

I'll know this worked when:

- The money question is answered, and my calendar is decided by calling instead
  of by rent.
- I have customers whose names I know, whose staff I've trained, who'd be
  annoyed if the product disappeared.
- I have a body of honest work online that points to God without shouting.
- Raizane starts as the obvious next step for customers I already have — not as
  project #17.

## 9. When I want to break the plan

I will want to. Around week 3, when the first "no" stings and a new idea feels
like relief.

Read this then:

> The idea is not the asset. The finished thing is the asset. I have proven I can
> start. I have not yet proven I can finish. Until I finish once, every new start
> is evidence against me, not for me.

Then put the idea in [`IDEAS.md`](IDEAS.md), close the file, and go make the
ten calls.

# The Daily Rhythm

This is the file I open every morning. The plan is for the month; this is for
today.

---

## The morning (30 min, before anything else)

1. **20 min — Word and prayer.** Phone in another room. Write one line about
   what I got. That line is also tomorrow's video if I want it to be.
2. **5 min — Look at the number.** Conversations this week (see
   [`SCOREBOARD.md`](SCOREBOARD.md)). Not revenue. Not views. Conversations.
3. **5 min — Write today's ONE outcome.** One sentence, finishable today.
   > Today is a win if: ______________________________

---

## Block 1 — SELL (2–3 hours, always first)

Before I build anything. Before I open the editor. Before I check anything.

- [ ] **10 real touches.** Walk-in, call, DM, or email — in that order of value.
- [ ] **Follow up every single person from yesterday.** Most sales are in the
      second contact and I have never once made a second contact.
- [ ] Run any demo that's booked.
- [ ] Log every conversation: name, place, what they said, next step, date.

**Best hours to walk into a hotel: 10:00–12:00.** Breakfast is done, check-out is
done, lunch hasn't started. The manager can actually talk.

> If I do nothing else today, do this block. A day with 10 conversations and no
> code is a good day. A day with beautiful code and no conversations is the last
> 15 months.

---

## Block 2 — BUILD (2–3 hours)

Only one rule, and it's the whole rule:

> **I only build what a paying customer asked for out loud.**

Not what I imagine they'd like. Not a refactor. Not a redesign. Not a new repo.
If nobody asked for it, it goes in [`IDEAS.md`](IDEAS.md) and I work on the
thing that was asked for.

If no customer has asked for anything yet: this block becomes more of Block 1.

---

## Block 3 — SHOW (45 min, hard stop)

- [ ] Film 1 video (or post from Monday's batch).
- [ ] Reply to every comment and DM. Every one. This is where customers come from.
- [ ] Post. Close the app. Don't check the numbers.

---

## The evening (10 min)

- [ ] Fill in today's row in [`SCOREBOARD.md`](SCOREBOARD.md).
- [ ] One line: what worked, what I avoided.
- [ ] Close the laptop. Thank God for the day, including the no's.

---

## Sunday — off

No building. No selling. No "just checking." Church, family, rest.

This is not a productivity hack. Six days is enough. The seventh belongs to God,
and the man who takes it is more useful on Monday than the one who didn't.

---

## Friday review (60 min) — the pressure valve

This is the **only** hour of the week where I'm allowed to think about Raizane,
Vox, POD, or anything in [`IDEAS.md`](IDEAS.md).

1. Fill in the weekly totals in [`SCOREBOARD.md`](SCOREBOARD.md).
2. Ask three questions and write the answers down:
   - What did customers actually say this week? (their words, not my summary)
   - What did I avoid doing, and why?
   - What is the single most important thing next week?
3. Read [`IDEAS.md`](IDEAS.md). Delete the ones that died. Keep the ones that
   didn't. Move nothing to "active."
4. Check [`PARKED.md`](PARKED.md) — has any wake-up condition been met?
   Almost certainly not. That's fine. Close it.

Knowing the ideas have a scheduled home is what lets me ignore them Monday
through Thursday.

---

## The three sentences

Stick these somewhere I'll see them.

> **Sell before you build.**
> **Finish before you start.**
> **God before both.**
